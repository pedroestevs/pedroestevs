Physics undergraduate at the **Federal University of Santa Catarina (UFSC)**, Brazil.
I work with observational astronomy and astronomical data: photometry, time series and catalogues.

## About

My main interests are stellar astrophysics and exoplanets, in particular the use of photometric, astrometric and time-series data to study stellar systems and their circumstellar environments.

Programming is part of the research itself, from processing survey catalogues to building reproducible analysis pipelines and the software around them.
